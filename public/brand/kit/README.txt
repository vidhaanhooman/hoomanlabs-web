HoomanLabs brand assets
=======================

Files
  hoomanlabs-logo-black.svg / .png   Full logo, for light backgrounds
  hoomanlabs-logo-white.svg / .png   Full logo, for dark backgrounds
  hoomanlabs-mark-black.svg / .png   Mark only, for light backgrounds
  hoomanlabs-mark-white.svg / .png   Mark only, for dark backgrounds

Use SVG wherever you can; PNGs are transparent, 1830px (logo) and 512px (mark) wide.

Name
  Write it as "HoomanLabs": one word, capital H and L.

Colours
  Ink    #171717
  White  #FFFFFF

Please
  - Leave clear space around the logo at least the height of the "H".
  - Keep the logo at least 80px wide on screen (mark: 16px).
  - Use the black logo on light backgrounds and the white logo on dark ones.

Please don't
  - Stretch, rotate, outline or add effects to the logo.
  - Recolour it, or place it on busy images without enough contrast.
  - Rebuild the wordmark in another typeface or combine it with other marks.

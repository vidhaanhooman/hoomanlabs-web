# "Painted sound" textures: ChatGPT prompts

Four abstract gouache textures, one per use case, for the "Hear an agent"
section. No scenes: just painted colour with brush texture. White ripple lines,
the voice bloom and white text are drawn on top in code, so keep them calm and
mid-toned.

Use the same ChatGPT chat as the other art (or upload
`public/hero/parallax/00-master.png` first as the style reference).

Save to `public/art/listen/` and tell Claude **"sound textures are in"**.

## Fix-ups

- **Too busy / blotchy:** *"Make the strokes longer, more even and more horizontal; calmer overall."*
- **Too dark or too bright:** *"Same image, but a mid-tone so white lines and white text on top are clearly readable."*
- **Colour off:** *"Keep everything the same, shift the colour to a muted [terracotta / ochre / sage / dusk blue]."*
- **It drew a scene:** *"No scene, no horizon. Only painted horizontal strokes of one colour."*

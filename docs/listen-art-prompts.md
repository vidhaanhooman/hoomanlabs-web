## 1. `listen-stage.png`

White text and a white circular graphic sit **in the centre**, over a dark scrim,
so this painting needs a calm, darker, low-detail middle and its interest at the
edges and bottom.

```
Next image: same gouache texture, brushwork and paper grain as the reference.

Resolution: 1536 x 1024 pixels, landscape (3:2). Fill the full canvas edge to edge, no borders.

SCENE: the wide valley at late dusk, the moment after sunset. A deep blue-grey sky that gets slightly lighter toward a thin band of warm amber just above the far hills. Across the valley, scattered farmhouses with a few small warm lit windows, as if people are on the phone. Along the LEFT and RIGHT EDGES: dark tree silhouettes and the slopes of nearer hills. Along the BOTTOM STRIP: dark meadow grass and wildflowers in silhouette, slightly more detailed.

COMPOSITION: the centre 60% of the image (where white text and a large white circle will sit) must be calm, simple and mid-to-dark in tone: open dusk sky and soft distant hills only. Keep the brightest light low on the horizon, not in the centre. Small lit windows only near the edges and lower third.

MOOD: quiet, warm, "someone is always on the line".

DO NOT INCLUDE: text, letters, numbers, logos, people, animals, cars, street lights, moon, neon, glow effects beyond small warm window light, lens flare, sci-fi gradients, borders or frames.
```

simple subject** that reads at a glance. Same style, close-up and still-life
rather than landscape, so they don't repeat the stage.

### `card-collections.png`
```
Next image: same gouache style as the reference, but a close-up still life, not a landscape.
```

## Fix-ups

- **Centre too bright or busy (stage):** *"Same image, but make the centre 60% darker and calmer; keep the light only low on the horizon and at the edges."*
- **Card too busy:** *"Simplify to one subject on a plain background; fewer objects."*
- **Style drifted:** *"Match the reference's gouache texture, brush marks and palette softness exactly."*
